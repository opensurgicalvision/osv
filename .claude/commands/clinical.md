Build a Clinical Lead review pack on `$ARGUMENTS` using the `clinical-review-pack` skill.

RC has roughly two hours a week. The pack is optimised for their time, not ours:

- **One specific question per item.** "Is this correct?" is not a question. "Does the duct
  boundary run above or below this fold?" is.
- Contested frames with the disagreeing masks overlaid, plus kappa/Dice for the batch.
- The relevant Annotation Guideline excerpt and any prior ruling from `docs/adjudications.md`.
- A recommended default per item, so RC can confirm rather than compose.

Do not pre-decide the clinical answer and do not present your own reading as consensus (R-14).
Record every decision back into `docs/adjudications.md`; ten similar cases trigger a guideline update.

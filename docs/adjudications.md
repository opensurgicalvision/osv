# Clinical Adjudications Log

Log of ambiguous anatomical boundary cases, contradictory annotations, and binding rulings by the Clinical Lead (RC).

## Protocol
1. An annotator/reviewer flags an edge case with `needs-clinician`.
2. Clinical Lead (RC) reviews the case within 7 days.
3. Decision is recorded here with anatomical rationale.
4. When 10 related adjudications accumulate for a specific anatomical class, `ANNOTATION_GUIDELINE.md` is updated.
